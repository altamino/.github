# AltAmino
![Status badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-maintained-green) [![Status badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/status_page-blue?logo=betterstack)](https://status.altamino.top)

AltAmino is project that trying to replicate Amino API servers. Amino was a social network for fandoms that is closed since December 2025.

Project mainly exist as attempt to save a piece of history making it working again allowing users feel the vibe, explore and learn what Amino is actually was while being able to expand features.

### Main repositories:
- [altamino/api](https://github.com/altamino/api)
- [altamino/websocket](https://github.com/altamino/websocket)

### FAQ

<details>
  <summary>What is our official instance?</summary>
  
  Basically any domain that linked to this "organization" account is our official domain. So for now it's [altamino.top](https://altamino.top).
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I delete any of my own data?</summary>
  
  For now just contact to developer team, provide email or AltAmino ID (basically Amino ID) and reason why you wanna delete your account (if you're european it can be itself as a reason).

  Also PLEASE when contact us, devs, consider use either English or Russian.

  (please help us with legal stuff)
</details>

<details>
  <summary>What is your position about AI?</summary>
  
  While AI can be helpful, it still can't replace a human, and companies can't scrape any data they want to train their AI models. We will do anything to at least try to protect artists since they are most vulnerable group.
</details>

<details>
  <summary>Can I be a developer too? / Can I develop [the thing you asking]?</summary>
  
  You can help our development for sure, we aren't against it. Maybe you can be a part of our team if you would constantly send good and clean PRs.

  We are also not against custom clients and bots **UNTIL** they are harmful. It's cool when you have spirit in the eyes, some impressive ideas, we can even support and verify them, but please make it safe for everyone else. Thanks in advance if you actually listened.
</details>
